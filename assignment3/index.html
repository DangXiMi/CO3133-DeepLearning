Progress (updated 24 Nov 2026)

Milestones: M1 Dataset Proposal — submitted. M2 Draft — complete. M3 experiments — complete. GMU ablation, missing-modality evaluation, actual joint multimodal fine-tuning, and error/support-conflict analysis. Final report, slides, and video are pending.

  • Task track: Image-text classification
  • Dataset: MM-IMDb — 23 classes, 25,959 pairs of movie posters and plot summaries. Exact duplicates found in the EDA were removed with priority test > dev > train. Final split: 15,553 train / 2,595 dev / 7,805 test.
  • Models:
    • Unimodal A: ResNet-18 and ResNet-50 on posters, fine-tuned.
    • Unimodal B: DistilBERT and BERT-base on plot summaries, fine-tuned.
    • Simple fusion: frozen image and text encoders, cached features, concatenation head.
    • Improved fusion: Gated Multimodal Unit (GMU), with and without modality dropout.
    • Joint fine-tuning: ResNet-50 and BERT unfrozen with encoder LR 1e-5 and GMU LR 1e-4.

Ablation: GMU without dropout is slightly better than with dropout on macro-F1: 0.588 vs 0.586. Missing-modality evaluation: image dropped 0.523 and text dropped 0.285 for GMU without dropout; image dropped 0.540 and text dropped 0.287 for GMU with dropout. Text is the stronger modality.

Actual joint fine-tuning: ResNet-50 and BERT unfrozen with encoder LR 1e-5 and GMU LR 1e-4, 3 epochs. This reaches macro-F1 0.597, micro-F1 0.670, sample F1 0.672, and exact match 0.177, improving over the frozen GMU by +0.008 macro-F1. Image dropped: 0.581; text dropped: 0.191.

Support vs conflict vs text-only, GMU without dropout: 570 movies where GMU is correct and text-only is wrong, versus 847 where GMU is wrong and text-only is correct. The result is better on average, not guaranteed on every movie.
